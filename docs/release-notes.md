An early version of Fruitfly for macOS 14 or later.

- One small fly above your apps.
- Food placement with a button or Option-double-click.
- Pause, hide, fly size, and screen controls.
- An included smell circuit from MaleCNS brain data.
- No account or separate data download.

Download the ZIP file. Extract it, then move Fruitfly to Applications.
The app supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

This download does not yet have Apple approval through notarization.
If macOS blocks the app, use Privacy & Security in System Settings to review the Open Anyway option.
See the README for build instructions.

The fly uses a small selected circuit. Food search and other pet behavior also use programmed rules.
Option-double-click reaches the app below. Use Place food to capture one click instead.
