# FlyPilot view

The main view is a flight workbench. The world fills the left two thirds.
The camera and cell plot share the right column. A strip below holds the errors. A small inset shows the path.
The controls stay above the view. The recorded canvas uses 1920 × 1080 pixels. Controls use verbs. Detail text explains the model.

Colors: charcoal #11191D, panel #202C31, text #E7ECEC, muted #9AADB5,
and blue #547985. The battle map uses dusty stone, exposed brick, dark vehicles,
and warm light through haze. The optional studio keeps its lighter palette.
Textured CC0 models add material detail. All cameras view the actual 3D scene.
The flight path uses blue. Active cells use coral on a blue anatomy plot.
Use Avenir Next for headings and the system sans serif for controls and values.
Align labels and data to the left. Keep large images free of decorative borders.

The distinctive element is the paired live camera and calculated cell plot.
Use one real scene and quiet controls. Do not add dashboard tiles or a marketing hero.
The page must work at laptop width. Stack the views on small screens.
Show model time, wall time, and replay speed as separate values.

The default battle view follows the drone and character. It starts wide enough to show
the doorway and cars. It moves closer as the drone arrives. The contact marker uses
the recorded body point. The 0.52 m drone is rendered at its actual simulated size.
